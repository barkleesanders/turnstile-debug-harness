## SECTION B: Notification of Vulnerability & Pro Bono Offer

I have identified that the current failure of the ITS GovQA FOIL portal is due to a misconfiguration in the Lanap BotDetect CAPTCHA (BotDetectCaptcha.ashx) causing session-affinity failures that reject valid users. I am prepared to provide pro bono assistance to remediate, including migration guidance to Cloudflare Turnstile as a modern, accessible alternative.

### Technical Details

**Bug:** The BotDetect CAPTCHA image renders with 0×0 pixel dimensions due to CSS layout collapse in DevExpress parent containers.

**Impact:** Users cannot see or complete the CAPTCHA, blocking form submissions.

**Fix:** Force explicit dimensions on CAPTCHA elements and parent containers.

### References

- **Turnstile product overview:** https://www.cloudflare.com/application-services/products/turnstile/
- **Repository with fix details and traces:** https://github.com/barkleesanders/turnstile-debug-harness (Commit b1aabe2 on main)
- **Technical analysis:** https://github.com/barkleesanders/turnstile-debug-harness/blob/main/docs/CAPTCHA_BUG_ANALYSIS.md
